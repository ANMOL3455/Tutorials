DR.Donut | Minecraft content creator

“Dr.Donut” (often written DrDonutt or Dr. Donut) is an American Minecraft YouTuber and live‑streamer best known as the owner and developer behind Donut SMP, one of the largest and most popular survival‑multiplayer servers in Minecraft.

Who he is ?

DrDonut’s real name is Nate Filson, a content creator from Virginia who rose to prominence in the Minecraft Crystal ‑PvP and SMP communities. He streams almost daily, often playing on his own Donut SMP world, where he built a server that now regularly pulls tens of thousands of concurrent players and sometimes surpasses even Hypixel in live player count.
